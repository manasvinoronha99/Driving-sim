🎭 Emotional Shadow Theater

Create expressive shadow stories through movement and emotion!

Your facial expressions change your shadow's color palette:

  • 😊 Happy/Excited: Warm oranges, reds, yellows
  • 😌 Calm/Peaceful: Cool blues, purples, teals
  • 😐 Neutral: Soft greens, earth tones
  • 😢 Sad: Deep blues, grays

Body movements create shadow characters that persist (outstretched arms = bird, crouched = mountain)

Interact with on-screen elements like trees, sun, and moon that also respond to your mood

Optional: Record 30-60 second emotional shadow stories
